Registration Restrictions for STAT A611: Graduate standing or instructor approval.
Course Description STAT A411: Introduces basic probability and statistics, including density functions, distribution functions, mathematical expectations, and moment generators for discrete and continuous random variables. Introduces regression analysis. 
Course Description STAT A611: Covers general probability and statistics, including density functions, distribution functions, mathematical expectations, and moment generators for discrete and continuous random variables. Covers regression analysis.
Attendance: Daily attendance is vital to success in this course. Frequently material is covered that is not in the textbook, for instance additional theorems, examples or applications, or different proofs of theorems. If you will be required to miss class for work or university business, including athletics, please inform the instructor at the beginning of the semester. 
Homework: A student cannot learn statistics simply listening to lectures and reading the textbook. The student must work through exercises to understand the material (the more the better). Each time a textbook section is covered a selection of exercises will be assigned. Solutions to all exercises will be provided via email regularly during the semester. You can also find the worked-out homework problems in the Course Content on Blackboard. Students are strongly encouraged to work through these exercises before their solutions are provided, and ask questions about them at the beginning of each lecture. 
Exams: There will be one comprehensive final exam at the end of the semester. It is an open book exam. You will be permitted to use a calculator on the exam. If a student misses an exam, it is his/her responsibility to take the exam at another date. Please arrange excused absences for exams before the exam if at all possible; otherwise make arrangements within one day of the exam. 
Grading Policy: The final exam grade will be your final grade in the class.
