What Statistics Does
Statistics is the science of:
• collecting data/information;
• analyzing, graphing, summarizing, and organizing data/information — descriptive statistics;
• drawing reliable conclusions from descriptive statistics — inferential statistics.

2. Sample Median
Notation: e𝑥.
• First order the dataset.
• If 𝑛 is odd, e𝑥 is the middle observation in the ordered dataset.
• If 𝑛 is even, e𝑥 is the average of the two middle observations.
Example:
1 5 6 9 10
1 2 3 4 5 =⇒ e𝑥 = 6.
Resistance to Outliers
The sample median is an example of a resistant measure: it is not strongly influenced by extreme outliers in
a dataset. The sample mean is not resistant.
